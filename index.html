A Letter To You

My Dearest Noor

There's an ocean between us,
Continents, time zones, and silence.

But no distance is greater
Than the space you fill in my heart.

I couldn't bring you a cake in person.
I couldn't hold your hand as you made a wish.
I couldn't watch your eyes light up
When the candles flickered.

But I stayed up nights building this world,
So you could feel — even from far away —
That someone thinks of you.
That someone celebrates you.
That someone loves you.

Happy belated birthday, Noor.
The best is yet to come.

— Ishaq
